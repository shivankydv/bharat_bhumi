Land Discovery Request

Know that you or your family may own land but don't have the documents or land details? Submit a request and the concerned authorities can help trace the possible land record.

help_center

Don't have your land documents?

That's okay. Provide whatever information you know about the owner, family member, village, district, approximate location, or land. Supporting documents are optional.

Step 1 — Applicant Information

Pre-filled from your profile where available.

Step 2 — Land Information

All land-identification fields are optional — share whatever you remember.

Step 3 — Supporting Information

Documents are optional. You can submit this request even if you have no documents.

Filenames are recorded for the demo; files stay on your device.

Step 4 — Review & Submit

Check all information entered.
