Document Help

  • Supported types: Sale Deed, Aadhaar / Identity Proof, RoR, Encumbrance Certificate, Tax Receipt, Mutation Application, Survey Sketch.
  • Upload PDF, JPG or PNG files. Keep each file clear and legible.
  • Pending means awaiting nodal-officer review; Rejected shows a reason — correct and re-upload.
  • Re-upload corrected documents from the Documents page; history is preserved.
Manage Documentsarrow_forward

Payment Help

  • Pay from Payments using UPI, Card or Net Banking against the Payment Due card.
  • Successful payments issue an instant e-receipt with transaction ID.
  • Pending usually means gateway settlement — wait 24 hours before retrying.
  • Failed payments are never debited twice; raise a grievance with the transaction ID.
Go to Paymentsarrow_forward

GIS / Map Help

  • Search parcels by ULPIN, owner name or locality in GIS Explorer.
  • Navy markers are property locations; green centre = verified, saffron = under review.
  • Use + / − to zoom, drag to pan, and the satellite toggle for imagery.
  • Click any parcel for ownership, area, class and tax details.
Open GIS Explorerarrow_forward

Account & Login Help

  • Login trouble: check username spelling, reset via the login help option, or contact support.
  • Change your password regularly from Settings → Security (min 8 characters).
  • Sessions expire on logout; use “Logout from all other sessions” on shared devices.
  • Keep profile email and mobile updated for OTPs and status alerts.

Accessibility Help

  • Text size, high contrast and reduced motion are available in Settings.
  • English and Hindi language options apply across portal headings.
  • All actions are keyboard reachable with visible focus outlines.
  • Form fields carry labels for screen-reader friendly controls.
