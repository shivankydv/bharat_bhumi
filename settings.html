Privacy & Data

The portal stores only what is needed to deliver land services: your profile details, login session, preferences, and demo records you create (uploads, payments, applications) in this browser. Data is used solely for service delivery and is never sold.

Danger Zone — Close Account

Request closure of your portal account. This clears locally stored demo data and signs you out. This cannot be undone in the demo.
